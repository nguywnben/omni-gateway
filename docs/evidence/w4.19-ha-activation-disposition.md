## Decision

Wave 4 engineering is implementation-complete, but coordinated HA is **not activated**. This
repository contains no accepted HA activation record, and every deployment surface continues to
enforce one worker and one application replica. The safe supported topology remains:

- `OMNI_RUNTIME_MODE=standalone`;
- `WORKERS=1`;
- `OMNI_REPLICA_COUNT=1` or Helm `replicaCount: 1`;
- SQLite, PostgreSQL, or MongoDB as the selected durable backend;
- the lifecycle-owned in-process coordination implementation.

This is a deliberate failed activation gate, not an incomplete or implicit HA rollout.

## Retained activation blockers

1. Provider OAuth/device transactions in `anthropic.py`, `xai.py`, and `codex.py` remain bounded
   process-local state. They must move behind a shared one-time transaction boundary before a
   replica receiving the callback may differ from the replica that initiated the flow.
2. Credential batch idempotency reservations and credential-pool mutation locks remain
   process-local. They must be coordinated or the management plane must have an explicit
   single-writer topology.
3. The Redis quota transition performs a bounded `HGETALL`/sorted-set inspection up to the
   configured 100,000-record ceiling. Its worst-case production latency was not measured, so the
   ADR-008 p95 and throughput targets are unresolved.
4. The required Redis plus shared PostgreSQL/MongoDB, two-replica, partition/restart, audit/usage
   completeness, and rollback matrix did not run.

`SUPPORTED_HA_ACTIVATION_RECORDS` therefore remains empty. A later wave may remove these blockers
and add one exact, immutable activation record only after the same external topology passes every
ADR-008 objective. Merely supplying `REDIS_URL`, raising a worker/replica value, or passing this
synthetic harness can never activate HA.

## Rollback posture

The HA operator is dry-run-first. Its rollback plan drains coordinated admission, preserves durable
authority and evidence, deploys one standalone process, and verifies health, readiness, audit, and
usage. It never deletes durable identity, audit, trace, usage, or migration records. Since no
coordinated topology was activated in W4.19, no data-plane rollback was required.
